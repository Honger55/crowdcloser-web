What We Do

Complete Roofing Services
for Central Oklahoma

From emergency repairs to full storm restorations, Red Line Roofing handles every aspect of your project with the precision of a professional craftsman.

Roof Replacement

Full tear-off and replacement with premium GAF materials. We assess your roof's condition and recommend the best solution for your home and budget, backed by manufacturer warranties.

Hail Damage

Oklahoma hailstorms are no joke. We identify and document all hail damage, provide a detailed assessment, and restore your roof to pre-storm condition fast.

Storm Restoration

When severe weather strikes, we respond fast. Wind damage, fallen debris, missing shingles — we get your home protected and your roof restored quickly and correctly.

Insurance Claims

Navigating insurance is overwhelming. We work directly with your adjuster, document every detail, and make sure you get the full claim you're entitled to.

Roof Repair

Not every problem needs a full replacement. We handle targeted repairs — flashing, valleys, ridge caps, and leak fixes — that extend the life of your existing roof.

Gutters

Properly functioning gutters protect your foundation and siding. We install, repair, and replace gutter systems to keep water flowing away from your home year-round.
